Legal notice & privacy

Who runs this

Requirements DB is a personal, non-commercial project of Luis María González. Contact: use the contact details on joderestatodocogido.com.

What is stored, why, and for how long

  • Reading without an account: no account and no cookies. To apply the rate limit we keep a salted hash of your IP address (not the address itself) in counters that are deleted after two days.
  • Signing in (Google, through Clerk): your account identifier. We do not store your email or name in our database.
  • Contributions: the text you submit, the automatic review verdict and your account identifier, until you delete them. The text is sent to Google's Gemini API for review.
  • Projects and suggestions: the project names and the status you set on requirements, tests and accelerators, and the text of your suggestions, with your account identifier, until you delete them.
  • API keys: only a hash of each key, its name and last-use time, until you revoke or delete them.

Your rights

Signed-in users can delete their data at any time (“Delete my data”): this removes API keys, contributions, projects and suggestions and detaches your identifier from requirements you authored. Requirements already published remain, anonymously, under the Content Terms. For anything else, contact the address above.

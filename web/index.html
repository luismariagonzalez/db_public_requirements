          API keys

          Keys raise your rate limit and let your agent read, update your projects and send suggestions. Keep them secret: they are shown only once.

            Use it from your agent

            Public and rate limited (20 requests/minute without a key). Responses are JSON, or ready-to-paste Markdown with format=md, which ends with instructions telling your agent to record, in your project, what is done and what is still pending.

            curl "https://<this-site>/api/v1/requirements?category=messaging&max_priority=2&include=tests,accelerators&format=md"

            Priority scale: P1 essential (broken or unsafe without it) · P2 expected · P3 recommended · P4 nice to have · P5 differentiator. Priority is per category.

            • Read: /api/v1/categories, /api/v1/requirements, /api/v1/tests, /api/v1/accelerators (and /:slug)
            • With a key (Authorization: Bearer rq_…): /api/v1/projects, PUT /api/v1/projects/:id/items, POST /api/v1/suggestions; add project=<id> to a search to see what you already did and hide=done to skip it
